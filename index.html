Calculation model, angle definitions and references

Momentum and crystal

k = E / (ħc), ħc = 1973.269804 eV Å. Eout = Ein − ΔE. q = kin − kout. In the scattering plane, q∥ = kin cos α − kout cos β and qn = −kin sin α − kout sin β. In crystal coordinates, q = Rz(φ) Rx(tilt) (q∥, 0, qn). Fixed-L incidence control solves 2θ on the same outgoing branch.

H = q·a₁/2π, K = q·a₂/2π, L = q·a₃/2π. The r.l.u. view shows H/K/L coordinate space. For oblique axes, √(H²+K²+L²) is not the physical |q| and is not shown. Surface normal ∥ a × b. BZs use the primitive reciprocal lattice for the selected centering. H/K/L use the input lattice axes: primitive for rhombohedral cells, conventional otherwise. Arbitrary surface orientations and refraction are not included.

Spectrum interpretation

Relative intensity is a sum of area-normalized Gaussians multiplied by a Lorentzian resonance factor. D = √[(sin²πH + sin²πK)/2]; the dispersive peak center is 0.02 + A D eV and its weight is wD. Dispersion uses the elastic approximation for q. Resolution and intrinsic widths are combined in quadrature.

Polarization, matrix elements, multiplets, self-absorption, temperature, absolute counts and Poisson noise are not included. This is not a first-principles or Kramers–Heisenberg calculation.
